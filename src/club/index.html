Building Champions In the Water

Developing skilled, competitive, and confident swimmers from Kenya's grassroots up to national and international level. Four structured programmes led by certified coaches with a maximum 15:1 ratio.

About the Club

Visa Dolphins Swim Club exists to build champions in the water. Founded by Coach Japheth Onkoba and registered in 2026 under Kenya's Sports Act, the club develops swimmers from complete beginners to national and international competitors.

Our long-term vision is to grow the next generation of Kenyan swimming talent — building the new Kenyan Dunfords from the ground up. We are a bona fide member of the Kiambu County Aquatics Association (KCAA) in good standing.

We serve parents, swimmers aged 5–18+, coaches, and sponsors through four structured competitive programmes designed to take athletes from their first splash to the international podium.

Our Programmes

Four progressive levels designed to develop swimmers of every ability.

Novice

Ages 5–12 beginners. Mon–Fri, 4:30–5:30 PM. Fundamental skills & technique. KES 1,000/session.

Intermediate

Building on basics. Mon–Fri, 5:30–6:30 PM. Technique refinement & endurance. KES 1,000/session.

Elite

Advanced competitive. Mon–Fri, 6:30–7:30 PM. Competition prep & performance. KES 1,500/session.

Professional

National / international level. Mon–Sat, 7:00–9:00 AM. Elite performance & race prep. KES 2,000/session.
