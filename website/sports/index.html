About Visa Dolphins Sports

Visa Dolphins Sports is the equipment supply arm of the Visa Dolphins ecosystem, providing professional aquatic gear to clubs, schools, coaches, county programs, and individual athletes across Kenya.

We offer fair pricing, reliable sourcing, and custom bulk kits tailored to the needs of swimming programmes at every level — from learn-to-swim schools to national-level competitive clubs.

In partnership with Decathlon Kenya, we ensure access to quality equipment that supports proper technique development and competitive performance.
