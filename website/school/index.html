About SwimKenya School

World Aquatics SwimKenya School is a CBC-aligned swimming academy delivering school programmes and coach certification. Our mission is building a sustainable pipeline of swimming talent in Kenya using international coaching methodology aligned with World Aquatics.

We serve schools (Grade 1–9), teachers, lifeguards, and parents through a structured learn-to-swim programme that emphasises correct technique, water safety, and progressive skill development.

What makes SwimKenya different: full alignment with World Aquatics standards, the internationally recognised Ten Stages method, and seamless integration with Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum.

The Ten Stages Method

A progressive, skill-specific approach where students advance at their own pace and are recognised for every milestone achieved.

Class Format

30–45 minutes per session, maximum 15 students per class. Each stage is skill-specific with a strong emphasis on correct technique.

Self-Paced Progression

Students advance at their own pace through all ten stages. Every milestone is recognised and celebrated along the way.

CBC Integration

Fully aligned with Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (Grade 1–9). Direct curriculum delivery partnerships across Nairobi and Kiambu County.

Stage 10 Outcomes

Graduates can swim 300m freestyle, 100m backstroke, and complete a 100m individual medley — with endurance for any aquatic activity and core water-safety capability.
